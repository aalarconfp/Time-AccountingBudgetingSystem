# Glossary

## Clock Capacity

Elapsed time available in the reporting period.

## Corrected Tracked Time

Tracked source time after explicit corrections.

## Tracked Overlap

Time observed by more than one tracked source that would otherwise be counted
multiple times.

## Unique Tracked Time

Corrected tracked time after subtracting tracked overlap.

## Off-Device Life

Meaningful human activity outside the digital tracking sources.

## Analytical Activity

Source-observed activity used for analytical hierarchy and behavioral analysis.
It may overlap and therefore exceed elapsed clock capacity.

## Category

High-level analytical classification.

## Subcategory

More specific classification within a category.

## Domain

Life domain such as Physical, Vocational, Relational, Recreational,
Intellectual, Economical, or Spiritual.

## Energy

Energy mode such as Recovery, Deep, Active, Shallow, or Passive.

## Goal

Current canonical goal dimension:

- Investment
- Maintenance
- Consumption

## EDA

Exploratory Data Analysis.

Diagnostic layer for discovering patterns and taxonomy candidates.

## Standard Report

Small recurring KPI report intended for period-to-period review.

## Taxonomy Candidate

An analytical node identified by EDA for human review.

A candidate is not automatically an error.

## Attribution Removal

Correction to the ownership/attribution of observed time.

## Classification Removal

Removal of time from an overstated analytical classification.

## Historical Integration

Controlled process of adding a closed reporting period to the long-term
historical dataset.

## Budget

Future planning layer representing intended time allocation based on current
goals.
